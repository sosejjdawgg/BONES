♥ CARE GUIDE

THE BASICS
BONES NEEDS FOOD, WATER, REST, PLAY & LOVE. WATCH HIS SIX METERS IN DOGCAM — LET ANY OF THEM RUN DRY TOO LONG AND HE GETS SICK.

REFILL THE BOWLS
TAP THE WATER OR FOOD BOWL RIGHT IN DOGCAM TO POUR. HE'LL HELP HIMSELF WHEN HE'S HUNGRY OR THIRSTY — YOUR JOB IS JUST KEEPING THEM FULL.

PLAY FETCH
OWN A BALL? DRAG IT DOWN INTO THE CONTROL DECK AND LET GO — THAT'S SLINGTOSS. LAND IT ON THE WHITE CROSS AND THE CROSS POPS AND PAINTS ITSELF SOMEWHERE ELSE: THE CARPET, A SIDE WALL, THE FAR WALL. HE'LL CHASE IT DOWN AND BRING IT BACK EITHER WAY. OR TAP PLAY FETCH FROM HOME AND HE'LL START THE GAME HIMSELF.

WALKS & THE DOGPARK
TAP GO OUT FOR WORK, TRAINING OR THE BEACH — OR HEAD TO THE DOGPARK ONCE HE HITS LV.5. BARK AT SQUIRRELS, BIRDS & CATS TO SCARE THEM OFF — HE DOESN'T HURT ANYONE, THEY JUST DROP A BONE AND SCUTTLE AWAY. SURVIVE THE WAVES AND REACH THE RED EXIT TO BANK REAL XP BEFORE HE GETS OVERRUN.

THE DOGPARK ECONOMY
EVERY SCARED-OFF CRITTER DROPS ONE BONE. SPEND THEM BETWEEN WAVES ON UPGRADES OR RARE CHARMS — OR TAP THE BONES COUNTER ANY TIME TO TRADE SOME FOR XP, MONEY OR A BONE TREAT. CLEAR A WAVE INSIDE 60 SECONDS AND A BONUS CHARM UNLOCKS IN THE SHOP. KEEP AN EYE OUT FOR THE GOLDEN BIRD — CATCH IT FOR A BIG BONES PAYOUT.

THE PARK OPENS UP
FROM THE SECOND WAVE ON, EVERY CLEAR PULLS THE CAMERA BACK A STEP AND HANDS THAT SPACE TO THE VIEW — THE PARK GETS WIDER AND THE CONTROLS GET TIGHTER. IT ALL HAPPENS DURING THE SLOW-MOTION FINAL KILL, SO YOU SEE IT SETTLE. IT FINISHES OPENING UP AT WAVE 10 AND STAYS PUT FROM THERE, HOWEVER DEEP THE RUN GOES.

DOGPARK UNLEASHED
HOLD 5,000 BONES AND DOGPARK UNLEASHED UNLOCKS. SAME 10 WAVES, DOUBLE THE ENEMIES ON SCREEN AT ONCE, UNDER A DARKER NIGHT SKY WITH FIREFLIES DRIFTING THROUGH THE GLOOM — PICK REGULAR OR UNLEASHED EVERY TIME YOU HEAD OUT. THE PARK STAYS WRAPPED IN FOG OUTSIDE WHERE HE'S RECENTLY EXPLORED, SO GROUND HE HASN'T WALKED IN A WHILE FADES BACK INTO THE DARK. NIGHT MODE IN SETTINGS SWITCHES OFF THE WHOLE NIGHT LOOK MID-RUN IF YOU'D RATHER SEE IT IN NORMAL DAYLIGHT.

THE SWORD (DOGPARK UNLEASHED)
WAVE 2 OF A DOGPARK UNLEASHED RUN, SOMETHING FALLS TO EARTH AND BURIES ITSELF NEARBY. WALK UP AND PAY THE PRICE SHOWN TO CLAIM IT — FROM THEN ON HE CARRIES IT IN HIS MOUTH AND CUTS DOWN ANYTHING HE RUNS INTO. SHARPEN THE BLADE BETWEEN WAVES FOR MORE REACH AND DAMAGE, UP TO 5 TIERS. LEAVE IT UNCLAIMED TOO LONG AND LIGHTNING STARTS STRIKING IT WHERE IT STANDS.

FIRE
WHEREVER REAL FLAME BREAKS OUT, EMBERS DRIFT OFF ON THE BREEZE AND SETTLE, GLOWING WHERE THEY LAND — MOST DRAMATIC ONCE NIGHT MODE'S DARK IS ACTUALLY UP. ANY SQUIRREL OR CAT THAT WANDERS INTO THAT FLAME, OR GETS CAUGHT IN A LASER BEAM, HAS A SMALL CHANCE OF CATCHING ALIGHT ITSELF — DOUBLED WITH NIGHT MODE ON. A PANICKING ANIMAL ZIPS AROUND IN TIGHT, UNCONTROLLABLE CIRCLES AT DOUBLE SPEED, ABLE TO SET STILL MORE TREES ALIGHT BEFORE IT FINALLY BURNS ITSELF OUT.

WHIRLWIND SLASH
ONCE THE SWORD IS IN HIS MOUTH, SPIN THE JOYSTICK ONE FULL FAST TURN AND HE CUTS LOOSE IN A WIDE, HEAVY CIRCLE — HITS EVERYTHING AROUND HIM FOR A GOOD WHILE AND KNOCKS BACK ANYTHING IT DOESN'T DOWN. LONG COOLDOWN, SO SAVE IT FOR WHEN HE'S SURROUNDED. UPGRADE WHIRLWIND MASTERY IN THE SHOP (UP TO 3 TIERS) TO WIDEN THE RING AND HIT HARDER.

RAGE & HEAVENLY JUDGMENT
TAKING HITS FILLS THE RAGE METER. ONCE IT'S FULL, TAP IT TO UNLEASH HEAVENLY JUDGMENT — THE WHOLE PARK SLOWS DOWN AS BOLT AFTER BOLT RAINS DOWN ON EVERYTHING NEARBY. A DEVASTATING PANIC BUTTON FOR WHEN THINGS GET OUT OF HAND.

FRIENDS
THE BANDANA DOG RUNS A SHOP SOMEWHERE OUT IN THE TREES — FOLLOW THE COMPASS OR JUST EXPLORE TO FIND HIM. RECRUIT SQUIRRELS, BIRDS, CATS AND (EVENTUALLY) AN APE TO FIGHT AT BONES' SIDE, EACH UPGRADEABLE THROUGH SEVERAL TIERS. THEY FLASH INVULNERABLE AND GET KNOCKED CLEAR WHENEVER THEY'RE HIT, SO THEY CAN SURVIVE A REAL FIGHT — IF ONE GETS HURT, TAP ITS ROW IN THE FRIENDS PANEL TO HEAL IT BACK UP FOR BONES.

ARMOUR & THE COMPASS
FULL ARMOUR (SHOP) SOAKS UP HITS BEFORE THEY EVER REACH HIS HEALTH. THE COMPASS (SHOP) POINTS TOWARD THE FRIENDS SHOP, THE EXIT, AND ANYTHING ELSE WORTH FINDING THAT'S CURRENTLY OFF SCREEN.

LEAVING A RUN EARLY
NEED OUT NOW? END RUN IN THE BOTTOM-LEFT OF THE CONTROLS TAKES YOU STRAIGHT BACK TO DOGCAM — BUT IT FORFEITS EVERYTHING EARNED THIS RUN, SO ONLY USE IT IF YOU REALLY HAVE TO.

SIDE MISSIONS
BARK AT EVERY KIND OF CRITTER IN ONE RUN FOR +20 XP. SURVIVE WAVE 1 FOR +10 XP. THESE PAY OUT STRAIGHT AWAY, NO NEED TO BANK FIRST.

BATH TIME
GRAB THE SPONGE OFF THE WALL AND DRAG IT ACROSS HIM TO SCRUB HIM SPOTLESS — YOU'LL NEED SHAMPOO FROM THE SHOP FIRST, AND EACH BATH USES SOME UP.

REST UP
A BED LETS HIM SLEEP ALL THE WAY TO FULL ENERGY — BUT IT HAS TO FIT HIM. EVERY TIME HE GROWS, HIS OLD BED BECOMES TOO SMALL AND HE ONLY TOPS OUT AT 70% AGAIN UNTIL YOU UPGRADE. TAP THE BED OR VISIT THE SHOP FOR A BIGGER ONE.

GROWING UP
EVERY BIT OF CARE EARNS XP. WHEN THE BAR FILLS, TAP IT TO LET HIM LEVEL UP — DON'T MISS LV.10, 25 & 50.

YOUR SPECIAL JOURNEY
ONE DAY BONES WILL BE A SENIOR. ONE DAY HE'LL NEED A SUCCESSOR TO CARRY IT ALL FORWARD. VISIT THE BREEDER WHEN THAT DAY COMES — THAT'S HOW THE FAMILY, AND THE LEGACY, GOES ON.

HE ONLY EVER NEEDS ONE THING FROM YOU: SHOW UP FOR HIM.
